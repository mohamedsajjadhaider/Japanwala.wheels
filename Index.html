My steering wheel vibrates at high speed

Diagnosis: Your wheels are likely out of balance.

Solution: You need our Computerized Wheel Balancing service. We will use precision weights to ensure a smooth ride.

My car pulls to the left or right

Diagnosis: Your wheel alignment angles (camber, caster, toe) are out of specification.

Solution: Book a 4-Wheel Alignment. This will straighten your steering and save your tires from premature wear.

My tires are wearing out on the inner/outer edge

Diagnosis: Improper camber or toe settings causing scrubbing.

Solution: Immediate alignment check. Bring your car to Japanwala Wheels before the tires are ruined.
